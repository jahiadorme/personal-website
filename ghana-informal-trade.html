Ghana’s GH₵7.4 Billion Informal Trade: Policy Imperatives for AfCFTA

Ghana’s GH₵7.4 Billion Informal Cross-Border Trade: A Mirror to Industrial Policy Failures and AfCFTA Readiness

Newly released data from the Ghana Statistical Service’s (GSS) inaugural Informal Cross-Border Trade (ICBT) survey for Q4 2024 reveal a parallel economy of striking magnitude: GH₵7.4 billion in unrecorded trade with Togo, Burkina Faso, and Côte d’Ivoire, equivalent to 4.3% of Ghana’s total trade.

Informal trade dominates Ghana's total commerce with Togo (61.2%) and Côte d’Ivoire (55.7%).
